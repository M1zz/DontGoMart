Before you head out,
check that the store is open

DontGoMart tracks the mandatory closing days of large supermarkets and Costco in Korea and reminds you before they shut. It keeps track of your favorite restaurants’ days off, too.

Key Features

🛒

Today’s status at a glance

Open the app and the first thing you see is whether your stores are open today, followed by a countdown to the next closed day and a list of upcoming ones.

🗺

Regional schedules

Pick stores that close on Sundays or Wednesdays, or regions with their own rules such as Ulsan and Jeju. The Costco Daegu, Ilsan and Ulsan locations each have their own setting, and Lunar New Year and Chuseok closures are included.

➕

Add your neighborhood store

If a store isn’t listed, add it yourself by choosing which weeks and which weekday it closes, for example the second and fourth Sunday.

🍜

Your go-to restaurants

Paste a link shared from Naver Map or pick a screenshot of the restaurant’s info page, and its regular days off are filled in for you. Text in photos is read on your device only.

🔔

Reminders that fit your routine

Get an alert 1, 2, 3 or 7 days ahead at the time you choose, plus an optional reminder the day before that today is a good day to shop.

📱

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

See the nearest closed day with a countdown, whether this Sunday is a closed day, or your restaurants’ next seven days without opening the app.

📅

Calendar and sharing

Browse a full month of closed days, and share the news as a card with a two-week calendar or as a short video.

Every feature is free

No more walking up to a closed store.
