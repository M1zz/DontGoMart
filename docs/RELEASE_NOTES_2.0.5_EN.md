# DontGoMart 2.0.5 Release Notes

## Bug Fixes
- **Fixed custom stores losing their selection**: Toggling a custom store off and back on silently deselected it — it looked enabled, but its closure days stopped showing on the main screen, widgets, and notifications. Now fixed.
- **Fixed the support products list being stuck on "loading"**: The tip products now load properly in Settings and on the support card.
- Deleting a custom store no longer leaves a stale selection entry behind.

## Improvements
- **The support card now appears on your custom stores' closure days too**: If the app saved you a trip on a day you configured yourself, you'll see the support card there as well. (Tapping "Later" still snoozes it for 30 days.)
