# DontGoMart 2.0.6 Release Notes

## New Features
- **Tips now trigger an instant thank-you celebration**: The moment a coffee, cake, or meal tip completes, confetti rains down, a thank-you card springs up, and a success haptic plays — the developer's gratitude, delivered instantly. (VoiceOver announcement included; with Reduce Motion on, the card shows without confetti.)
- **Supporter thank-you reminder**: If you've tipped before, the app occasionally (about 3 times a year) celebrates you again on a store closure day — a reminder that your donation hasn't been forgotten, with an option to send another tip right there.

## Improvements
- Unified the emoji labels on the tip product list (☕️🍰🍱).
